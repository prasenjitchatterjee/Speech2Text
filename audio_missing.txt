We would like to provide a brief clarification regarding the intermittent issue where small portions of audio (sometimes at the beginning or mid-conversation) are not transcribed when using the Whisper Large v3 Turbo model.

After detailed analysis and parameter tuning, we have confirmed that this is a known behavior reported by multiple users across OpenAI and Hugging Face platforms, and is not specific to our implementation.

Below are documented references:

1. Skipped Sentences / Random Missing Segments
Users reporting Whisper randomly skipping sentences:
https://community.openai.com/t/whisper-sometimes-randomly-skip-sentence/533502

2. Missing Audio Sequences After Processing
GitHub discussion highlighting missing portions (e.g., 15–20 seconds not transcribed):
https://github.com/openai/whisper/discussions/1193

3. Chunks of Speech Omitted in Longer Recordings
Reports of Whisper leaving out sections in long-form transcription:
https://community.openai.com/t/whisper-leaves-out-chunks-of-speech-in-longer-transcript/715999

4. Hugging Face Discussion on Untranscribed Segments / Blank Outputs
Community thread discussing missing or incomplete segments during chunked transcription:
https://huggingface.co/openai/whisper-large/discussions/13

These discussions indicate that:

Whisper processes audio in ~30-second windows and relies on chunking/stitching for long recordings.

Long-form decoding uses internal confidence thresholds and fallback mechanisms.

In certain edge cases (boundary speech, silence, noise, abrupt transitions), valid speech segments may occasionally be skipped.

We have optimized chunk size, stride overlap, and GPU batch handling to significantly reduce occurrence. However, due to the underlying model design, rare edge cases cannot be fully eliminated.

We continue to monitor updates from OpenAI and Hugging Face and will incorporate improvements as they become available.

Please let us know if you would like a technical walkthrough of the mitigation measures in place.
