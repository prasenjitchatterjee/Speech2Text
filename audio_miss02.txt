We would like to provide a clear explanation regarding the intermittent issue where small portions of audio (occasionally at the beginning or mid-conversation) are not transcribed when using the Whisper Large v3 Turbo model.

After thorough investigation, tuning of parameters (including chunk size and stride overlap), and validation across multiple datasets, we confirmed that this behavior is a known limitation of the Whisper architecture and its long-form transcription mechanism, and is not specific to our implementation.

Below is a concise explanation with references from official and community sources.

1. Architectural Limitation – 30-Second Processing Window

Whisper models are designed to process audio in ~30-second windows. Any audio longer than this must be handled using chunking or sequential long-form transcription logic.

This is explicitly documented in the Hugging Face model documentation for Whisper:
https://huggingface.co/openai/whisper-large-v3

Because longer audio must be split and reassembled, there is an inherent dependency on window stitching logic.

2. Long-Form Transcription Uses Heuristics That Can Drop Segments

For long audio, the model relies on timestamp prediction and internal heuristics such as:

Log probability thresholds

Compression ratio checks

Temperature fallback

These mechanisms are meant to prevent hallucinations and low-confidence outputs, but in some edge cases (noise, silence, overlapping speech, boundary cuts), they may suppress valid speech segments.

This behavior is documented in the Hugging Face Whisper implementation details:
https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers/en/model_doc/whisper

3. Known Segmentation & Boundary Issues in Community Discussions

Several users have reported skipped or partially transcribed segments when using chunking or long-form processing:

Hugging Face discussion on Whisper Large v3 Turbo:
https://huggingface.co/openai/whisper-large-v3-turbo/discussions

OpenAI Whisper GitHub discussions highlighting long-form edge cases:
https://github.com/openai/whisper/discussions

Community discussion describing skipped chunks during transcription:
https://community.openai.com/t/whisper-asr-model-skipping-chunks-in-audio-transcription/1067744

These reports confirm that the issue is observed across environments and deployments.

4. Why Parameter Tuning Helps but Cannot Guarantee 100%

We adjusted:

chunk_length_s

stride_length_s (overlap)

GPU batch processing

These measures significantly reduce the likelihood of missing segments. However, due to the underlying window-based architecture and heuristic filtering, there is no deterministic guarantee that 100% of edge-case boundary speech will always be captured.

This is a known trade-off between:

Avoiding hallucinated or duplicated text

Maintaining stability in long-form transcription

Managing performance at scale

5. Current Status

The model performs reliably in the vast majority of cases.

The issue appears intermittently under specific acoustic conditions (e.g., boundary speech, low SNR, abrupt starts, long silences).

It is consistent with known behavior documented by OpenAI and Hugging Face communities.

We continue to monitor updates from the Whisper and Transformers maintainers and will evaluate improvements as they are released.
